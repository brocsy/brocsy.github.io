What We Do

The Torridge Male Voice Choir is based in North Devon, performing a wide repertoire ranging from traditional choral pieces and spirituals to modern popular songs. We regularly perform at concerts, charity events, and community gatherings throughout Devon.

Join Us

Singing is fun! It's good for your health and very sociable. We are a friendly group of men of all ages and abilities. If you are looking for an opportunity to sing in the North Devon area and want to join us, please send us a message.

We meet at Woolsery Village Hall on Wednesday evenings at 7:45 PM. You would be made very welcome, no audition required!
